Data
Everything lives in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
Journal a day
1Open Calendar and click any day (past or today).
2Pick an emoji — a one-tap read on how the day felt.
3Add trades: symbol, side, contracts, entry, stop, target, exit. P&L auto-calculates from entry → exit × $/point.
4Tag the setup and session, tick the rules you kept, write the lesson, drop chart screenshots.
5Save entry. Dashboard, Insights and Gallery update instantly.
The numbers, decoded
WIN RATEwinning trades ÷ all trades
PROFIT FACTORgross profit ÷ gross loss — above 1.00 = net profitable
EXPECTANCYaverage $ result every time you take a trade
R MULTIPLEprofit measured in units of risk (entry → stop distance)
MAX DRAWDOWNdeepest fall from an equity peak in the range
DAY STREAKconsecutive green (W) or red (L) days, from your latest day back
Sessions & setups
Setup tags name the play (ORB, VWAP Fade…); session tags name the clock (Asia, London, NY Open…). Tag both on each day and Insights shows which plays pay and which hours bleed. Both lists are fully yours — add or remove them in Settings → Journal.
Make it yours
Settings → Appearance: dark/light theme, any accent color (pick a preset or dial in your own with the color picker), background glow strength, default chart. Settings → Journal: your assets (any market — futures, forex, anything with a $/point), setup tags, starting balance, weekend days. Profile: your name and an optional password that locks the journal behind the login screen.
Charts & the Gallery
Drop chart screenshots or short screen clips onto any day under SCREENSHOTS & CLIPS. Every image and clip is collected in the Gallery, tagged with its date, mood and P&L. Click any thumbnail to open it full-screen, or jump straight back to that day's journal.
Speed & shortcuts
Use the Day / Week / Month / All range switch to reframe every stat at once. Hit Today on the Calendar to snap back to now. Hover the equity curve for a day-by-day balance readout, and toggle Curve / Bars to switch the P&L view. Click any past or current day to journal it instantly.
Risk sizing
Set Starting balance, account risk percentage and each symbol's dollars per point in Settings → Journal. Entry and stop then produce a whole-contract size guide. Apply is always explicit, and risk sizing is informational, not financial advice.
Time edge and weekly review
Add an optional execution time to reveal results by hour in Insights. The Weekly Review Digest summarizes the latest seven calendar days. Tilt status is behavioral pattern detection, not a market prediction.
Search
Use Search in the title bar or press Ctrl or Cmd plus K outside a form field. Search finds journal entries, actions, saved presets and pages. Presets change only the open draft until you save the journal entry.
Chart markup
Choose Mark up on a screenshot to place Entry, Stop, Target, Mistake or Lesson markers. Undo, clear or cancel the working copy, then save markup to the draft. Saved markers also appear in the Gallery viewer.
Walkthrough
Run the four-step tour again any time — it covers search, journaling a day, shaping the dashboard, and turning records into a process. Nothing is changed or cleared.
Your data
Written is a local application — everything is stored in this webview on this device. Nothing is uploaded, ever. Logging out is a privacy curtain, not a server session. Settings → Data can reload the demo journal or wipe everything (both ask twice). Hover the P&L graph for day-by-day balance; set a starting balance to see your real account curve.

V1.0.0 · LOCAL BUILD · JUL 2026
A futures trading journal that treats journaling as part of the trade. Log the numbers, the mood, the setup, the session and the rules — then let the dashboard connect what you did to what you made.
Written runs as a local application inside this webview. There is no server, no account and no telemetry — your entire ledger lives in this device's storage and never leaves it.
